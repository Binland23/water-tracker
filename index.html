      How drinks count

      Pure water counts 100%. Other drinks only count a portion toward your goal (coffee ~80%, soda ~75%, tea/sports ~90%, iced latte ~89%, juice/smoothie ~85%) because they are not pure water. ELECTROLYTES still counts as the water you poured (1:1) — sticks are not extra fluid ounces. The app tags them and plays a charge animation.

      Data stays on this device only. Add to Home Screen from Safari for the full-screen app. Siri Shortcuts can open ?add=250 to log water by voice — see README.
